### Resource Description
This playlist is the full collection of videos from the 'Digital Connections' / Digital Citizenship series. It is a core component of the Common Sense Digital Citizenship Curriculum for Middle School. The content is designed to be relatable and engaging, using a conversational and vlog-like style to provide visual explanations for the key lessons taught in grades 6, 7, and 8.

**Key Topics Covered:**
* **Digital Footprint & Identity:** Understanding how personal information is shared and tracked online, and how online actions create a lasting impression.
* **Online Relationships & Communication:** Navigating the complexities of communicating through screens and social media; exploring the impact of digital life on social-emotional health.
* **Cyberbullying & Digital Kindness:** Strategies for being an upstander, standing up for others, and maintaining a positive online community.
* **News & Media Literacy:** How to identify 'clickbait' and evaluate the credibility of online information.
* **Privacy & Security:** Lessons on password protection, data collection, and protecting personal information.
* **Media Balance:** Tips for managing time and energy between the digital and physical worlds.

**Instructional Use:**
These videos are designed to be shown in class to spark discussion and introduce the vocabulary of digital citizenship before diving into the deeper lesson activities. They can be used as standalone conversation starters or as part of the full interactive curriculum found on the Common Sense website. They provide the 'hooks' necessary to engage 11-to-14-year-olds in serious discussions about ethics and technology.
