### Resource Description
Following the provided screenshot, this guide is paired with **Video 3** of the Family Toolkit. It focuses on the intersection of AI technology and academic responsibilities.

**Key Objectives:**
* **Thoughtful Engagement:** Guides families on how to help children use AI as a collaborator in their learning process.
* **Responsible Usage:** Emphasizes using AI tools in a way that aligns with school values and personal growth.
* **Effectiveness:** Provides strategies to ensure AI is used to enhance understanding of schoolwork rather than just generating answers.
