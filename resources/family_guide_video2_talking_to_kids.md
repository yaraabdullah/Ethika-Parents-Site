### Resource Description
Based on the provided screenshot, this guide is designed to be paired with **Video 2** of the Family Toolkit. It focuses on deepening the family's engagement with the social and moral implications of technology.

**Key Objectives:**
* **Continuous Dialogue:** Encourages families to maintain ongoing, meaningful conversations about technology.
* **Core Ethical Pillars:** Specifically focuses on teaching families about **Ethics, Privacy, Fairness, and Responsibility**.
* **Value-Based Learning:** Helps parents guide their children in understanding how AI impacts their rights and social values.
