### Resource Description
This playlist is specifically curated for parents and caregivers. It moves away from technical definitions and focuses on **practical parenting strategies**. 

**Key themes covered in the videos:**
* **Safety & Privacy:** How to protect children's data while using AI tools.
* **Academic Integrity:** Managing the use of AI in homework (building on the 'AI for schoolwork' guide).
* **Social Impact:** How AI affects children's creativity and social interactions.
* **Practical Tips:** Short, snackable advice for busy parents to stay informed without feeling overwhelmed.
