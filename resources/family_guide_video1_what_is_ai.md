### Resource Description
This guide is designed to be paired with **Video 1** of the Family Toolkit. Its primary goal is to help families:
* **Start Conversations:** Bridges the gap between classroom learning and home discussion.
* **Interactive Activities:** Includes short, hands-on tasks to demystify how AI functions.
* **Better Understanding:** Simplifies complex concepts so both parents and children can grasp the basics of AI together.
