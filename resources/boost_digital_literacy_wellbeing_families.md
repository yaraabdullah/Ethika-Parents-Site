### Resource Description
This resource acts as a bridge for educators to provide families with high-quality, research-backed activities. It focuses on several key pillars of digital citizenship:

**Key Components:**
* **Media Balance:** Tools like the 'Digital Life Emoji' to reflect on how technology makes us feel.
* **Safety and Privacy:** Games and activities to teach children about protecting personal information.
* **Relationships & Communication:** Guidance on navigating online interactions and preventing cyberbullying.
* **Digital Well-Being:** Practical steps to ensure technology use supports, rather than hinders, mental and physical health.
