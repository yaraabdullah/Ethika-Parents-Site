### Resource Description
This video featuring Merv Lapus from Common Sense Media addresses the common concern of 'how much screen time is too much' by providing age-based guidelines and practical tips for creating healthy digital habits.

**Key Age-Based Guidelines:**
* **Preschoolers (2-5):** Limit to 1 hour per day [00:00:44] of high-quality programming; watch together to help them make real-life connections [00:01:08].
* **Kids (6-9):** Focus on educational content that builds life skills like curiosity and emotional awareness [00:01:27]. Ensure tech doesn't interfere with sleep or outdoor time [00:01:43].
* **Pre-teens and Teens:** Focus on autonomy and non-judgmental interest [00:02:14]. Help them identify how online activities make them feel [00:02:29].

**Categories of Screen Time:**
* **Passive:** Mindless scrolling or watching videos [00:03:01].
* **Interactive:** Video chatting, problem-solving, or playing together [00:03:08].
* **Creative:** Using tech to create music, art, or writing [00:03:17].

**Top 5 Tips for Families:**
1. **Set Expectations:** Create a Family Tech Plan together [00:03:41].
2. **Prioritize Quality:** Choose content that encourages thinking and creating [00:04:12].
3. **Watch/Play Together:** Use screen time as a bridge for conversation [00:04:30].
4. **Aim for Balance:** Ensure tech doesn't displace physical activity or sleep [00:04:58].
5. **Lead by Example:** Model healthy tech habits as an adult (e.g., no phones at mealtimes) [00:05:17].
