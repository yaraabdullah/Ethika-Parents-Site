### Resource Description
'Digital Connections' is an interactive video experience designed to teach middle school students essential digital citizenship skills. The series follows two teens, Daveed and Skylar, as they navigate common digital dilemmas.

**Core Features:**
* **Interactive Format:** Students don't just watch; they make choices that affect the outcome of the story, allowing them to see the consequences of digital actions in a safe environment.
* **Targeted for Middle School:** Specifically addresses the social and emotional challenges of students in grades 6-8.
* **Bilingual Accessibility:** All videos and interactive elements are available in both English and Spanish, making it an ideal resource for diverse classrooms and families.
* **Educator Support:** Includes a dedicated Educator Guide to help teachers integrate the videos into their lessons and facilitate classroom discussions.

**Key Learning Themes:**
* Navigating online relationships and communication.
* Understanding digital footprints and privacy.
* Responding to cyberbullying and promoting digital kindness.
* Balancing time online with other life activities.
